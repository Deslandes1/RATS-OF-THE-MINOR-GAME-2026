ANDROID TOUCH & DESKTOP RTX · 3-STORY MANOR & FOREST

RATS OF THE MANOR

Explore and battle across a full 3-Story Manor (Floor 1 at 0.0m, Floor 2 at 6.0m, Floor 3 at 12.0m, roof at 18.0m) with walkable staircases and landings between every story, surrounded by a vast Forest (-94m to +94m) with winding trails, clearings, and dense pine & oak trees.

Android & Touch Controls: Use the left virtual thumbstick to walk/run/climb, drag anywhere on the screen to aim & turn, and tap 🔥 FIRE, ⚔ LUNGE, 🦸 FLY, or ▲ JUMP!
Desktop Controls: Move with WASD, aim with Mouse, shoot with Left-Click, fly Superman-style with F, and lunge at rats with G.
